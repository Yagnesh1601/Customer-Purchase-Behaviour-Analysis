AEGIS STATION · BIOLOGICAL CONTAINMENT PROTOCOL

OUTBREAK ANALYST

An unknown pathogen is moving through the orbital research station. You are the Chief Scientific Officer. Clean the records, read the evidence, test the treatment, forecast the wards, and contain the outbreak — with data.

HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Contain the outbreak aboard Aegis Station. Each mission puts a real data-analysis problem in front of you. Make the best decision you can with the evidence, and file your result to Command.

Mission order

Missions must be completed strictly in order, from 1 to 10. Only Mission 1 is open at the start. A mission stays locked (shown with a padlock) until the one before it has been completed. Completing Mission 10 completes the game.

Completion & scoring

A mission is completed when you submit your final decision and receive its debrief. Each debrief gives a score from 0 to 100 and a rank. Replaying a mission keeps your best score. Your average score is taken over the missions you have completed this session.

S · 90+A · 75+B · 55+C · below 55

Station integrity

Integrity starts at 100 and drops with every mission you file below a perfect score, so careful analysis keeps the station safe. Scores and progress last for the current session only: opening the game again starts a new run at Mission 1.

Navigating the game

  • Central Command is your hub. Click an unlocked numbered node on the station ring, or use Begin next mission, to launch a mission.
  • The Decks list on the left jumps between Central Command and any unlocked mission. Locked missions show a padlock and will not open.
  • Request Intel in the transmission panel gives a hint for the current room. The speaker control mutes sound or changes volume.
  • After each debrief you can retry the mission, return to Central Command, or move on to the next mission.
  • The ? button in the top bar reopens these instructions at any time without affecting your progress.

Ten missions, ten skills

Every mission tests a different data-analysis or outbreak-management concept, from cleaning raw records to coordinating the final containment.
